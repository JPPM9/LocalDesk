# LocalDesk

A desktop app for Windows that lets you use AI models **on your own computer** – through
[Ollama](https://ollama.com). No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

- **Chat** with local models, with projects, templates and skills
- **Knowledge:** your documents (PDF, Word, Markdown, code) searchable in chat, with sources
- **Obsidian:** memories and notes in your vault
- **E-mail (Gmail):** read, summarize, suggest sorting – changes are made only after your
  confirmation; nothing is ever sent or deleted
- **Code area:** an agent working in a project folder – changes shown as before/after,
  commands only after your approval

## Download

Find the installer under [Releases](https://github.com/JPPM9/LocalDesk/releases). LocalDesk
needs [Ollama](https://ollama.com/download) to be installed.

## Privacy

Your data stays on your PC. LocalDesk has no server and no account, collects no usage data and
sends nothing to third-party AI services. Details: [Privacy policy](privacy.md) (English and German).

The Gmail feature only reads and labels your mail on your own computer, after you set up your
own Google app. See the [e-mail setup guide](email-setup.md).
